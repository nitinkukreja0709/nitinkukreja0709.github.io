About

I build and ship large language models for India's national payments infrastructure. At the National Payments Corporation of India, I work across the lifecycle of FiMI, NPCI's 24B-parameter financial LLM — from data and multi-stage training to RL post-training, evaluation and agentic deployment in UPI Help and MyUPI.

My interests are post-training (SFT, preference optimisation, RL with verifiable rewards), RL environments for tool-using agents, and evaluating LLMs for safety and reliability in high-stakes domains.

Selected work

FiMI — a 24B domain-specific financial LLM

NPCI · arXiv:2602.05794

  • Led post-training and agentic integration; +20% over the Mistral Small 24B base on finance reasoning and +87% on domain tool calling.
  • Multi-stage training (CPT → IFT → SFT) on 68B curated English, Hindi and Hinglish tokens, distributed across 64 H100 GPUs with DeepSpeed ZeRO-3.

FiMI Banking — RL post-training for safe bank agents

NPCI · arXiv:2609.03960

  • Preference optimisation and verifiable-reward RL for multi-turn banking tool use: out-of-scope refusal 52% → 80% with 29% fewer tokens.
  • Built Banking Gym, a deterministic RL environment with a stateful bank simulator, confirmation-gated writes and verifier-graded rewards.

IndicBankBench — evaluating banking LLM assistants

NPCI · arXiv:2609.29167 · code

  • 799 multi-turn banking cases across 20 axes with deterministic safety and tool-use gates; 11 models from 8 families evaluated.

UPI Help & MyUPI — agentic assistant in production

NPCI · launched at Global Fintech Fest 2025

  • Multi-agent backend (LangGraph, MCP, FastAPI) for UPI disputes, mandates and escalation in 5 languages, with RAG / Graph-RAG grounding and guardrails.
